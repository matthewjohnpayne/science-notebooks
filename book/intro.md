# Science notebooks

Jupyter notebooks by [Matthew Payne](https://matthewjohnpayne.github.io/) that explore the dynamics and methods behind his research papers, with each result reproduced by direct computation.

- **Real, verified physics.** Every figure is computed in the notebook, from the equations and data the paper used, and compared with the paper's numbers where it can be.
- **Cited precisely.** Each notebook names the paper it follows; where it differs from the paper, it says how and why.
- **Readable and runnable.** The pages here show each notebook with its stored outputs. To run one yourself, clone the [repository](https://github.com/matthewjohnpayne/science-notebooks) and follow its README; long calculations have a quick mode and cached results.

Many notebooks have an interactive version that runs in your browser, listed on the [Teaching page](https://matthewjohnpayne.github.io/teaching/) of Matthew's website. For working with Minor Planet Center data and services, see the [MPC tutorials](https://docs.minorplanetcenter.net/tutorials/).
