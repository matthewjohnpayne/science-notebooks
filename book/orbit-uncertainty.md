# Orbital uncertainty

How the uncertainty of an asteroid's orbit, and of where it will be seen, grows, shrinks and changes shape as observations accumulate, and how it looks from different places. Unlike the other notebooks here, these are not each tied to one paper. They share a small toolkit, `orbunc`, built on the ASSIST integrator.
